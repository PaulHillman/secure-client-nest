# Dashboard: jump menu + collapsed previews

## The jump menu at the very top

One row of links, in the same order the sections appear down the page. Admin-only entries are hidden from students.

Admin sections:
- Gaps (the four number tiles)
- Students by team
- Client contacts
- Project arch
- Meeting gaps
- Meeting consensus
- Sign-in / Sign-out log
- File upload activity
- Team activity dashboard
- Cross-team comparison
- Flagged students
- Student activity score

Everyone:
- My profile
- My availability
- My role
- Role practice activities (admin only)

Clicking a link scrolls to that section and opens it if it is collapsed.

## What every list section looks like by default

Title at the top, with the total count next to it (for example "Students by team (76)"), plus a "Show all" button on the right.

Below the title: the first 5 rows only, with their column headings. Filters and controls stay visible so the section still makes sense at a glance.

Under the preview: a line like "Showing 5 of 76 — Show all".

Clicking "Show all" expands to the full list and the button becomes "Show less". Nothing loses data; only the number of visible rows changes. The choice is remembered while you stay on the page.

## Which sections get this treatment

Collapsed to 5 rows by default:
- Students by team
- Sign-in / Sign-out log
- File upload activity
- Team activity dashboard
- Cross-team comparison
- Student activity score
- Flagged students
- Client contacts
- Meeting gaps

Left as they are (not row lists, or already short):
- The four number tiles
- Project arch
- Meeting consensus
- My profile, My availability, My role
- Role practice activities
- Getting started

## Notes

- Sections with fewer than 6 rows show everything and no "Show all" button.
- Printing is unaffected.
